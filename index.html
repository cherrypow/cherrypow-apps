About

ThaiLang is a free, offline Thai language learning app. Whether you're planning a trip to Thailand or starting from scratch, ThaiLang helps you build vocabulary, learn grammar, and practice with interactive games.

  • Grammar lessons with progressive difficulty
  • 100 essential Thai phrases by category
  • Word bank with shuffle and random drills
  • 9 games: Sentence Builder, Tense Transformer, Word Match, Flashcards, and more
  • Audio pronunciation and phonetic transliterations
  • XP progression system
  • No ads, no sign-up, fully offline

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 24, 2026

ThaiLang does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device with no server communication.

Data collection: None. ThaiLang does not collect any personal information, usage analytics, crash reports, or device identifiers.

Third-party services: None. ThaiLang does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Local storage: Your progress and settings are saved locally on your device and are never transmitted externally.

Children's privacy: ThaiLang does not collect any data from any user, including children.

Changes: If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
