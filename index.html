Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 24, 2026

All Avelaval Inc. applications — ThaiLang, ManLang, JapanLang, and Mnemo — do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All apps run entirely on your device with no server communication.

Data collection: None. Our apps do not collect any personal information, usage analytics, crash reports, or device identifiers.

Third-party services: None. Our apps do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Local storage: Your progress and settings are saved locally on your device and are never transmitted externally.

Children's privacy: Our apps do not collect any data from any user, including children.

Changes: If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
